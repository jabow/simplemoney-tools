# Archived branding — Simple Money Tools

Superseded marks, kept for reference. One folder per generation, oldest first.
Current mark: **3D Hazel** — `assets/branding/logo/` (site-wide from 30 Sep 2026; see `../README.md`).
The flat vector Hazel it grew from is in `../hazel-flat/`.

| Folder | Mark | Chosen | Superseded | Why it went |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `01-chart-arrow/` | Original chart-arrow mark, soft green / pale blue, layered tones | pre-Sep 2026 | 13 Sep 2026 | "a bit too geeky" |
| `02-medallion/` | Chart-and-ribbon illustration inside a pale-blue coin | 14 Sep 2026 | 15 Sep 2026 | "not distinctive" |
| `03-squirrel-v1/` | Pastel squirrel in profile hugging a £ coin | 15 Sep 2026 | 26 Sep 2026 | too soft / "babyish"; replaced by Hazel |
| `05-hazel-rounds/` | Hazel design rounds 5–7 that led to the flat mark (see `HAZEL-README.md` inside) | — | 26 Sep 2026 | working files |
