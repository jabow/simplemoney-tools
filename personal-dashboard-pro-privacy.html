Personal Dashboard Pro — Privacy Policy

What the Personal Dashboard Pro Chrome extension does, and does not do, with your data.

Last updated 12 September 2026 · Applies to Personal Dashboard Pro version 2.0 and later. Published by James at Simple Money Tools, correspondence address available on request, United Kingdom. Contact: SimpleMoneyTools1@gmail.com. This policy covers the Personal Dashboard Pro Chrome extension only — for this website, the free Budget Tracker and the free Personal Dashboard extension, see the site-wide privacy policy.

This page is not legal advice, and has not been reviewed by a solicitor. It is written to be cautious and accurate about what the extension actually does, and every statement below was checked against the extension's source code.

For the purposes of UK data protection law, James at Simple Money Tools is the data controller for the limited information described in "What we receive" below. For everything the extension stores on your own device, we are not able to access it and hold no copy of it.

The short version

  • There is no account and no sign-up for Personal Dashboard Pro.
  • Your dashboard settings, bookmarks, watchlist and any integration credential are stored in your browser's own extension storage, on your device. We do not receive them and hold no copy.
  • We operate one small proxy service that sits between the extension and certain data providers. It receives a rounded location, ticker symbols and photo keywords, plus the technical information any web request carries, including your IP address. That is the only information that reaches us.
  • The Trading 212 and Strava integrations are optional, off until you connect them, and request read-only access only. The extension makes no write requests to either service.
  • No advertising, no sale of personal data, and no analytics or tracking code in the extension.
  • You can disconnect an integration, or delete every trace of local data, at any time, on every tier, free.

What stays on your device

Personal Dashboard Pro has no user accounts, no sign-up and no login, and we do not operate a user database for it. The following are stored in Chrome's local extension storage on the device where you installed it, and are not synced to your Google account, copied to your other devices, or transmitted to us:

  • Dashboard settings and layout preferences
  • Bookmarks you add, and your watchlist
  • Any Trading 212 API key or Strava token you have chosen to connect
  • Cached portfolio, activity, price and weather responses
  • Your anonymous ExtensionPay licence key

A note on local security. Local extension storage is protected by your operating system and Chrome profile, not by additional encryption we apply. Anyone with access to your unlocked device and Chrome profile, or to a device backup that includes it, could read what is stored there. Treat a connected Trading 212 API key with the same care you would treat any other credential, and disconnect it if you share or dispose of the device.

What we receive

Some dashboard data comes from providers that require an API key. Shipping those keys inside the extension would publish them to anyone who unzipped it, so the extension instead calls a small Cloudflare Worker we operate, which adds the key server-side before forwarding the request.

The Worker has no user accounts, no login and no per-user state. It is not sent anything that identifies you by name or email, and it cannot associate two requests with the same person.

What never passes through the proxy: your Trading 212 data, your Strava data, any credential or token, your bookmarks, your browsing history, your name or your email address.

The Worker caches provider responses at its edge so repeat requests share one upstream call. It caches the response, not the requester.

Where this policy says a rounded location is used, that rounding happens on your device, so a precise location does not leave it. A location accurate to roughly a kilometre, combined with an IP address, may still be personal data, which is why it is listed here rather than described as anonymous.

Trading 212 (optional, read-only)

The integration is off until you deliberately connect it, and works with an API key you generate yourself inside your own Trading 212 account.

  • Where the key is stored: on your device only, in local extension storage. It is not synced, not sent to us, and not included in an exported settings file — the export is built from an explicit allow-list of permitted fields.
  • What it is used for: two read-only requests — your account summary and your open positions.
  • What the extension does not do: it makes no write requests to the Trading 212 API. It does not place, cancel or modify orders, and does not deposit, withdraw or change account settings. It requests only the two read endpoints named above.
  • Where the request goes: directly from your browser to Trading 212's API. It does not pass through our proxy, and no third party sits in between.
  • Account types: the Trading 212 API covers Invest and Stocks ISA accounts. SIPP and CFD accounts are not exposed by that API and are not supported.

Fetched portfolio data is cached locally so the dashboard does not re-request it on every new tab. That cache stays on your device, is not synced, and can be cleared on its own from Settings → Privacy.

Your use of the Trading 212 API is also governed by Trading 212's own terms and privacy policy.

Strava (optional, read-only)

Also off until you connect it, and authorised through Strava's own sign-in page using OAuth.

  • Your password: entered on Strava's own website. The extension never sees it and never stores it.
  • Scope: read-only access to your activities and profile statistics. No write scope is requested, and the extension makes no write requests — it does not post, edit or delete activities or change anything on your Strava account.
  • Tokens: access and refresh tokens are stored like the Trading 212 key — locally only, not synced, not exported.
  • Where the request goes: directly from your browser to Strava's API, not through our proxy.
  • Disconnecting: removes the stored tokens from your device and ends the extension's access.

Figures the extension works out are not Strava's figures. The fitness estimate, the activity streak and the weekly trend rows — Activities, Activity time, Swim, Bike and Run — are calculated by Personal Dashboard Pro from the activity data it reads. They are labelled as the extension's own calculations wherever they appear, are not official Strava metrics, and are not medical, health or fitness advice. Do not rely on them for any health decision.

Activity data can reveal information about your health or physical condition. It is read into your browser, used to render your dashboard and cached on your device. It is not sent to us and we hold no copy of it.

Your use of Strava is also governed by Strava's own terms and privacy policy.

Other connections the extension makes

These go directly from your browser rather than through the proxy, which means the provider concerned can see your IP address in the ordinary way that any website you visit can.

  • Cryptocurrency prices from CoinGecko's free public API. No key is required, so there is nothing for the proxy to protect.
  • Background photo images from the photo provider's own image servers, once the proxy has chosen which photo to show.
  • Bookmark icons from Google's public favicon service, which is sent the address of each bookmarked site in order to return its icon. If you would rather not share bookmark addresses with Google, you can turn icons off in Settings → Appearance.
  • Trading 212 and Strava, as above, if and only if you have connected them.

Background photos

Photos come from Unsplash, with Pexels as a fallback when Unsplash's shared hourly quota is exhausted. Each photo is credited on the dashboard to its photographer and source. Neither provider is sent anything about you beyond the topic keyword and the technical data any web request carries.

On the free tier the extension fetches up to five new photos per day and then reshuffles recent ones. This is a cost control on a shared API quota, not a data-collection mechanism — the count is a number in your own local storage.

International transfers

Our proxy runs on Cloudflare's global network, and the providers named above (Cloudflare, Unsplash, Pexels, CoinGecko, ExtensionPay, Google's favicon service, and Trading 212 and Strava if connected) may process requests on servers outside the UK, including in the United States. Where we transfer personal data outside the UK we rely on the UK International Data Transfer Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, or another transfer mechanism permitted under UK GDPR, as set out in the relevant provider's data processing terms.

Payment

Personal Dashboard Pro is a one-off £5 purchase handled by ExtensionPay on its own hosted checkout. Card details are entered on ExtensionPay's page and never reach the extension or us — we do not see, receive or store them. Depending on how you complete checkout, ExtensionPay may hold an email address for your licence; where it does, ExtensionPay is the controller of that information under its own privacy policy, and we use only the anonymous licence key held locally to ask whether this installation has been paid for.

Disconnecting and deleting your data

Either integration can be disconnected at any time from Settings → Privacy. Disconnecting removes that integration's stored credential from your device.

The same panel has a one-click "Delete all local Personal Dashboard Pro data" control, which permanently removes every connected account, every stored credential and every cached record from your device. It asks you to confirm first. It is free for every user, not a Pro feature — a paywall in front of deleting your own data would not be acceptable.

Uninstalling the extension also removes its local storage, as Chrome does for any extension.

What this extension does not do

  • No advertising, and no ad networks.
  • No selling, renting or sharing of personal data.
  • No third-party analytics, telemetry, tracking pixels or usage reporting. There is no analytics code in the extension.
  • No reading of your browsing history, and no content scripts on the pages you visit. The only content script runs on ExtensionPay's own checkout page, to confirm a completed purchase.
  • No fabricated figures. A metric that is genuinely unavailable is shown as unavailable, and one you have chosen to hide is shown as hidden — the two are always distinguished.

We use Chrome Web Store user data in line with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Cookies

The extension sets no cookies of its own and has no cookie banner, because it has nothing to ask consent for. Third-party sites you open from it, including ExtensionPay's checkout, are governed by their own policies.

Children

Personal Dashboard Pro is not intended for, or directed at, anyone under 18, and connecting a Trading 212 or Strava account requires an account with those providers under their own age terms. We do not knowingly collect information relating to children. If you believe a child has used the extension in a way that involves us, contact us at the address above.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict and object to the processing of your personal data, the right to data portability, and the right to withdraw consent where processing relies on it.

In practice, almost everything Personal Dashboard Pro handles is stored on your device and under your own control, and you can delete all of it yourself at any time using the control described above. The only personal data we receive is the limited proxy request data described in "What we receive". Because that data is not linked to an account or any identifier we hold, we are usually unable to identify which requests are yours, and may not be able to act on an access or erasure request without further information that would itself identify you — UK GDPR does not require us to collect such information in order to comply.

If you wish to exercise a right, or you believe this description does not match your situation, contact SimpleMoneyTools1@gmail.com and we will respond within one month.

You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office, the UK supervisory authority, at ico.org.uk or 0303 123 1113. We would appreciate the chance to address your concern first.

Security incidents

If we become aware of a security incident affecting personal data we hold, we will assess it and, where required, report it to the ICO within 72 hours and notify affected users. Because we hold no dashboard data, portfolio data, activity data or credentials, an incident affecting our systems would not expose them.

Changes to this policy

We aim to update this page before a change to the extension's data handling ships, and will in any event update it promptly and revise the "last updated" date above. Material changes will also be flagged in the extension. If a future version introduces server-side storage, accounts or a subscription, this policy will be revised before that version is released.
